# Better Home LinkedIn Launch Pack V1

Status: Ready for review  
Primary market: Adelaide, South Australia  
Primary audience: homeowners, builders, architects, interior designers and property professionals  
Primary language: English (Australia)

## Launch principle

Do not begin with “our new website is live”.

The first four posts establish the belief, the life moments and the Product System. The fifth post then gives people somewhere useful to continue.

Publishing sequence:

1. Why Better Home exists
2. Technology should quietly support everyday life
3. A home is made of moments
4. Introducing the Better Home Product System
5. Explore and build your Better Home

Recommended rhythm: two posts per week over three weeks.

The Meng Zhang personal profile should publish Posts 1, 2 and 4. The Better Home Technology company page should publish Posts 3 and 5. The other account may reshare each post with a short personal comment rather than duplicating the complete text.

## Post 4 — Introducing the Better Home Product System

Channel: Meng Zhang personal profile  
Purpose: Explain how Better Home becomes a purchasable product  
Suggested image: A simple four-part graphic or a photograph of the Collection Book and A4 Product Sheets together  
Link: Products page, once production is live

### Copy

How do you turn a residential philosophy into something a customer can understand, choose and buy?

We built the Better Home Product System around four clear decisions.

**1. Foundation**  
One shared core for the home. Selected once.

**2. Collections**  
Choose the spaces and routines that matter: Entry, Living, Kitchen, Bedroom, Bathroom and Away.

**3. Experiences**  
Add atmosphere, climate, healthy air, garden care or professional monitoring where it belongs.

**4. Add-ons**  
Extend something already selected—another curtain, lighting zone, sensor or compatible device.

An Add-on does not invent a new room or a new experience. It simply gives an existing experience more coverage.

This structure allows a customer to begin with one part of the home, understand the standard scope and price, and add more over time without rebuilding the system.

It also keeps the same product truth across our Collection Book, Product Sheets, website, Shop, proposals and installation workflow.

Simple for the customer.  
Clear for the sales team.  
Consistent for delivery.

## Visual direction

Every launch image should feel like it belongs to the same Better Home system:

- Warm neutral or restrained forest palette
- Real residential material and natural light
- Minimal or no text over the image
- No floating UI, blue technology glow or phone-control imagery
- Prefer authentic project photography wherever possible
- If a concept image is used, do not imply that it is a completed Better Home installation

Recommended LinkedIn landscape size: 1200 × 627 px.  
Recommended carousel page size: 1080 × 1350 px.

## What to measure

Do not judge the launch by likes alone.

Track:

- Visits to `/better-home`
- Product pages viewed
- Visits from Products to Shop
- Shop selections started
- Selections submitted
- Builder or designer conversations started
- Existing contacts who reply directly to Meng

The most valuable early signal is not reach. It is whether the right people continue from the idea into the Product System.
