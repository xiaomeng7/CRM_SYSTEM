# Evidence Guidelines v1 (Pilot Batch)

## Directory Use
- `buyer/`: buyer and pre-purchase evidence plans
- `suburb/`: suburb-specific evidence plans
- `builder/`: builder and handover evidence plans

## Image Naming Examples
- `buyer-adelaide-switchboard-overheat-202605-01.jpg`
- `suburb-norwood-oldwiring-defect-closeup-202605-02.jpg`
- `builder-adelaide-handover-panel-labeling-202605-01.jpg`

## Metadata Examples (per image)
- `source`: site inspection / technician note / installer photo
- `date`: 2026-05-11
- `location`: Adelaide / Norwood / Unley
- `issueType`: switchboard | grounding | legacy wiring | compliance
- `usageScope`: insights-only | service-page-ok
- `privacyReview`: approved | pending

## Alt Text Examples
- "Older Adelaide switchboard with missing modern safety switch protection identified during pre-purchase inspection."
- "Close-up of aged cable insulation in a Norwood roof space, flagged as medium-risk electrical defect."
- "Builder handover panel in Adelaide custom home showing incomplete circuit labeling before final QA."

## Rules
- Do not fabricate defects, suburbs, pricing, or case details.
- Do not use placeholder imagery to support factual claims.
- Keep all evidence references traceable to source notes.
