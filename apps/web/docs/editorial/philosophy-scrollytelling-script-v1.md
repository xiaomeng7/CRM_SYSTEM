# Better Home Philosophy  
## Editorial Narrative Script v1  
### (Scrollytelling Structure Draft)

---

# PROJECT PURPOSE

这不是：

一篇网页文章。

而是：

一场被 scroll 驱动的 residential philosophy experience。

目标：

让观看者不是“了解 Better Home 是做什么的”，
而是感受到：

- calm
- thoughtful living
- spatial rhythm
- emotional comfort

以及 architecture + light + technology 如何共同形成更好的生活体验。

---

# OVERALL TONE

不是：

- futuristic
- flashy
- luxury flex
- smart-home demo
- tech showcase

而是：

- restrained
- architectural
- cinematic
- emotional
- human-centered
- slow
- atmospheric

---

# PACING PRINCIPLES

整个页面不应该一直有内容。

必须有：

- silence
- darkness
- breathing room
- pauses
- emotional transitions
- reveal moments

---

# CHAPTER 1 — Homes Should Feel Human

## Emotional Goal

让用户放慢节奏。

感受到：

安静与空间感。

## Visual Direction

- dim architecture
- low contrast
- warm darkness
- partial reveal
- soft shadows
- quiet material textures

不要：

- 明亮全景
- showroom lighting
- busy composition

## Narrative Flow

Homes should not simply function.

They should support the way people live.

Quietly.  
Naturally.  
Calmly.

---

# CHAPTER 2 — The Problem With Modern Homes

## Emotional Goal

很多现代住宅 technically advanced，
但 emotionally uncomfortable。

## Narrative Flow

Harsh downlights where softness was needed.

Single switches that erase nuance.

“Smart” scenes used once and forgotten.

Beautiful architecture undermined by systems bolted on too late.

Technology alone does not create comfort.

---

# CHAPTER 3 — Living Rhythm

## Emotional Goal

表达住宅应具备生活节奏感。

## Narrative Flow

Morning clarity.

Evening warmth.

Quiet paths at night.

Rest where rest is needed.

Light should support rhythm.

Not dictate it.

---

# CHAPTER 4 — Light As Environment

## Emotional Goal

灯不是产品，而是环境的一部分。

## Narrative Flow

Light shapes emotion.

It guides attention.

It creates calm.

It allows architecture to breathe.

A well-lit home should rarely announce itself.

---

# CHAPTER 5 — Beyond Technology

## Emotional Goal

Better Home 不是 smart-home gadget company。

而是 living systems philosophy。

## Narrative Flow

Technology should disappear into the living experience.

Comfort.  
Light.  
Access.  
Climate.  
Presence.

Not isolated devices —
but one coherent environment.

---

# CHAPTER 6 — Closing

## Emotional Goal

安静收束。

## Narrative Flow

The best residential systems do not demand attention.

They simply make life feel calmer.

Clearer.

Easier to live in.

---

# IMPLEMENTATION NOTES

页面必须：

- 慢
- 克制
- 安静
- cinematic
- editorial

不要：

- heavy animation
- startup UI feeling
- excessive transitions
- tech demo visuals

动画仅允许：

- fade
- slow reveal
- opacity transition
- subtle movement
- restrained parallax

目标：

让页面像一本缓慢展开的建筑杂志。

不是产品页面。
不是 documentation article。
不是 SaaS landing page。
